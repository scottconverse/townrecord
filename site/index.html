One record, two workspaces

Useful to the person keeping it.
Open to the people who need it.

For community operators

Set up public bodies and supported sources. Retrieve meeting material, check its origin, correct dates or attachments, and publish with a reason. New acquisitions stay private until you review and republish.

Windows setup and daily operation →

For residents and reporters

Browse published meetings without an account. Search available text, filter by body or date, read documents, and follow timestamps to the original recording. Missing text stays an explicit gap.

From a public meeting
to a newsroom lead.

Export a published meeting as evidence JSON. TownReporter can receive its documents, available transcript and source links as an unpublished lead. Importing the same revision preserves existing newsroom edits.

Your data, on your infrastructure.

Python service. TypeScript interface. SQLite and local artifact storage. HTTP/JSON access and verified backup/restore. Operators do not need Node to run the packaged interface.

Local transcription uses Whisper small through TextFlowKit. Provider access and caption availability vary; the connector capability guide explains the boundaries.
