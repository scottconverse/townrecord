## Why no out-of-order boundary is added

The brief's fix is conditional on the item being dropped. Adding it anyway would mean
writing, on 9.B, a reason saying it was heard out of order, when the placement the code
made is a real reading of that item in the transcript. That is inventing a fact.

The order-break drops that do exist on this fixture carry `_RULE_ITEM_NUMBER`, not
`_RULE_SPOKEN_IDENTIFIER`:

```
  item_number: 6 candidates, items ['4.A', '9.']
      '4.A' at 3972.4 s: 'item A, first and'
      '4.A' at 8009.6 s: 'item A?'
      '4.A' at 8012.2 s: 'Item A'
      '4.A' at 8606.2 s: 'Item A, purchasing decision'
      '9.' at 3704.2 s: 'Item 9 is ordinance'
      '9.' at 3717.9 s: 'Item 9 is ordinance'
  total order-break candidates: 6
```

`_longest_orderly_chain` (spec 10.2) gives each item one contiguous window, so a second
hearing of an item cannot be a second window; the only way to move 9.B into the 8040 to
8220 s block is to choose which of its many mentions anchors it. That is the decision the
brief leaves open, and it is the coordinator's, not mine.

## The question

**When an item is named several times in the transcript, which mention anchors its
boundary: the earliest reading of it (today's rule, 3680.9 s for 9.B), or the latest one
at which it is moved and voted (8086.4 s for 9.B)?**

A one-line answer is enough. The two candidate rules, and what each costs:

* **Keep the earliest reading.** No change; 9.B stays at 3680.9 s with an empty reason.
  Its motion and vote at 8072 to 8220 s is unrepresented, and the item's window does not
  cover the part of the meeting the coordinator is looking at.
* **Prefer the latest identifier mention when the item is moved and voted.** 9.B would
  start near 8086.4 s, and something has to be stored as its reason ("heard out of
  order", or the rule's name). The rule would apply to every item rather than to 9.B
  alone, so the placements and the counts by method would have to be re-measured on this
  fixture and on the others that carry captions.

If the answer is the second, the brief's Part 3 text ("if it is dropped, allow an
out-of-order boundary ... and store a reason that says the item was heard out of order")
can be reissued unchanged for that half; the identifier-spelling half is already done.
